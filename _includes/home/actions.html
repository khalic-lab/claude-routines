{%- comment -%} Discuss: a plain link that works without script. The template writes a short prompt (a story's headline and primary source; an editorial's title) and assets/js/discuss.js rebuilds the href once at load from the text on the page, so the page carries no second copy of its prose. `data-discuss` names the kind. It sits at the end of the fold, after the sources: quiet, where a reader who has read the story finds it (C16). Opening the conversation reads the item, like opening its article (board.js). {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.s -%} {%- assign st = include.s -%} {%- capture dq -%}Discuss this news story with me: {{ st.headline }}{% if st.url and st.url != "" %} Source: {{ st.url }}{% endif %}{%- endcapture -%} {%- comment -%} url_encode writes a space as "+", which only a form-style decoder turns back into a space; "%20" reads as a space under either decoder, and a literal "+" is already "%2B" by then. {%- endcomment -%} Discuss this story with Claude (opens in a new tab) {%- elsif include.e -%} {%- assign ed = include.e -%} {%- capture dq -%}Discuss this AI-written editorial with me: {{ ed.title_text | default: ed.title_html | strip_html }}{%- endcapture -%} Discuss this editorial with Claude (opens in a new tab) {%- endif -%}
