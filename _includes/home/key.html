AI editorial: opinion, not reporting

Position is the ranking: the front page first, then each day’s edition, biggest story first. A tag like “Science · 15–21 Oct” is the span that desk’s edition reports on. Read stories dim where they stand; they never move or shrink. Under Unread the front refills with the most important stories you have not read. “Single source”, “Preprint” or “Disputed” is the desk’s own flag on a claim; “3 sources” says how many outlets it cites, and opening the story lists them. Discuss starts a Claude conversation from a story, an editorial or a whole day.
