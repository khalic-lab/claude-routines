- <a id="st-3cb6384e2a9e" class="st-a"></a>**Unauthorised demonstrations in Zurich and Basel on the night of Saturday 31 October ended in rioting, with barricades set on fire in both cities.** In Zurich, several hundred people, some in Halloween masks, gathered on the Langstrasse at about 22:30 under the slogan «On en a marre – en Suisse aussi» ("We've had enough – in Switzerland too"), an echo of the youth protests in France and Germany. City police said a "violent mob" attacked officers with fireworks, stones and bottles, and police answered with a water cannon, irritant gas and rubber bullets. A car was set alight, and shop windows and the Stauffacher tram stop were smashed. Police also said a crowd attacked a patrol called to help a teenager badly hurt in an accident, forcing the officers to pull back before they could reach him. According to the police, left-wing extremist groups had called the Zurich march. In Basel, cantonal police said about 150 people marched through the city centre without a permit, public transport had to be halted, and protesters built and burned barricades on St. Johanns-Platz. Why it matters: this is the first time the protest wave sweeping France and Germany has led to violence in Swiss cities.
