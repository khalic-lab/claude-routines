- <a id="st-f8e811198902" class="st-a"></a>**Russia launched a massive missile and drone attack on Ukraine's power grid from the evening of Tuesday 13 October into Wednesday 14 October, two days after US President Donald Trump announced an "energy ceasefire" that Moscow never confirmed.** Ukraine's Air Force counted 39 missiles, most of them on ballistic trajectories, and 128 drones. It said it downed four ballistic missiles and 115 drones, and recorded hits at 22 locations. Russia's Defence Ministry said it struck two power-and-heating plants in Kyiv, two more in the surrounding region, a substation, a heating plant in Kharkiv and a data centre in Kyiv. Al Jazeera counted at least six people killed across the country, three of them in Kyiv. More than half a million people lost power. He said partners gave no early warning of the ballistic launches. His Air Force spokesperson later said warnings did come for three later waves, but not the first. Zelensky also told NPR that Washington had threatened to cut intelligence sharing unless Ukraine stopped drone strikes on Russian refineries. Why it matters: Trump announced the "energy ceasefire" on 11 October without either side confirming it, and the heaviest strike on Ukraine's energy system this autumn has now tested it. Kyiv also says US pressure is limiting its own long-range strikes. ## 🇨🇭 Switzerland & Vaud

- <a id="st-8bbb69aef005" class="st-a"></a>**The Federal Council on Wednesday 14 October opened a consultation on amending the Constitution so that adults with intellectual disabilities are no longer barred from voting in federal matters.** Today, people under a general guardianship (curatelle de portée générale) or a mandate for incapacity on grounds of lasting incapacity of judgement lose their federal political rights. The change to Art. 136 of the Constitution and the federal political-rights laws would let them vote in National Council elections and federal votes, and sign initiatives and referendums. Parliament asked for the change in motion 24.4266. Several cantons have already dropped equivalent rules. The consultation runs until 28 January 2027. Because it changes the Constitution, the reform will need a mandatory vote of people and cantons. Why it matters: it would end one of the last categorical exclusions from the Swiss vote. It is part of a wider overhaul of adult-protection law that aims to abolish general guardianship. — [Conseil fédéral / admin.ch, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.admin.ch/fr/newnsb/E7kYfiMOZOmH)

- <a id="st-6c2138014820" class="st-a"></a>**The Federal Council on Wednesday 14 October received Switzerland's third national assessment of money-laundering and terrorist-financing risks, which finds overall exposure stable since 2021 but flags crypto, fintech and wealth management as high-risk.** The report comes from the federal interdepartmental coordinating group on money laundering (GCBF). It names organised crime, corruption, property crime and tax offences as the main threats, mostly with an international dimension. It places the highest residual risks in wealth-management banks and "other banks", especially crypto banks. It also rates as high risk fintech firms, virtual-asset service providers, money transmitters, trustees, legal advisers, fiduciary companies and real estate. "Residual" risk means the risk left after existing controls. It recommends more resources for supervisors and prosecutors, better databases, and a review of how well the reporting system, precious-metals controls and the new transparency register work. Why it matters: it is the official map of where Swiss authorities think dirty money gets in, and it comes a day after the US fined Bank of America over the same kind of failings (see World). — [Conseil fédéral / admin.ch, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.admin.ch/fr/newnsb/Amio6afANTmb); [GCBF report summary (PDF), 14 Oct 2026](https://cms.news.admin.ch/fileservice/sdweb-docs-prod-nsbcch-files/files/2026/10/14/2847c405-5969-4983-839b-ff6b25407ad0.pdf)

The national economic-supply organisation will name which products are covered. Reports will go through the existing electronic Therapeutic Products Platform. The consultation runs until 28 January 2027, and the duty is planned to start on 1 August 2027. Why it matters: supply disruptions have multiplied in recent years. Without the reports, Bern has no overall picture from which to decide on emergency measures such as releasing stocks. — [Conseil fédéral / admin.ch, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.admin.ch/fr/newnsb/CjMfSkioYMfq)

- <a id="st-b2cf61676e21" class="st-a"></a>**The Federal Council said on Wednesday 14 October that health insurers should not be required to put all their investment income towards lowering premiums, and that the current voluntary mechanism is enough.** In its report answering postulate 22.3930 (Feller), it says insurers may already deduct a share of capital income from the costs charged to basic insurance, and most do when their solvency is good. Keeping it voluntary avoids extra premium swings and lets insurers use the income to build reserves when needed. Why it matters: for policyholders facing another premium rise, the government has declined to make insurers' investment income a guaranteed source of premium relief. How much relief there is still depends on each insurer's choices. — [Conseil fédéral / admin.ch, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.admin.ch/fr/newnsb/k9VijbvpdXiJ)
