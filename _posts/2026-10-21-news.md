- <a id="st-e6b19079265a" class="st-a"></a>**The UN Environment Programme said on Wednesday 21 October that current policies put the world on course for up to 2.8°C of warming this century, and that overshooting the 1.5°C limit can no longer be avoided.** Even if every national climate plan (NDC, a country's pledge under the Paris Agreement) is fully delivered, warming would reach 2.6°C. Why it matters: the UN's own yardstick now says the 1.5°C goal can only be met by overshooting it and coming back, which sets the terms for this year's climate summit. Why it matters: firms still relying on short-time work lose the extended cushion gradually through 2027 rather than all at once, and the government can still pause if the outlook worsens.
