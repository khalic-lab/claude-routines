- <a id="st-99d032661403" class="st-a"></a>**Federal President Guy Parmelin announced on Friday 2 October that he will step down from the seven-member Federal Council (Switzerland's collegial national executive) on 31 December, triggering a by-election in parliament.** The 66-year-old Vaud winegrower, who heads the economics department (EAER) and is serving his second year as rotating Federal President, said the decision has nothing to do with his health — persistent rumours about his back had circulated for years — and that "no one is irreplaceable" and the moment had come "to make way for fresh forces." His departure opens a seat the Federal Assembly will fill in a December vote, with the SVP expected to claim it. **Why it matters:** the resignation reshuffles the federal executive just as parliament is handling the Bilaterals III treaty package with the EU and a tariff dispute with Washington — files Parmelin personally carried — and sets off a succession contest for one of the country's seven top jobs.
