- <a id="st-95740335a9bb" class="st-a"></a>**Swiss health-insurance premiums will rise 5.0% on average in 2027, the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) announced on Tuesday 29 September.** The mean monthly premium climbs to CHF 412 (+CHF 19.70); adult premiums rise 4.9% to CHF 487.60, young-adult premiums 4.8% to CHF 338.80, and children's 4.6% to CHF 128.10. The government attributes the increase to a higher volume of treatments, medical-technology advances, and an ageing population, all feeding cost growth in the compulsory basic insurance (OKP — the mandatory health cover every resident must buy). The rise re-accelerates after a milder 4.4% increase for 2026 (mean CHF 393.30), though it stays below the 6.0% jump for 2025. Why it matters: the premium is a flat per-head levy that does not scale with income, so a 5% rise weighs most on lower- and middle-income households; the Federal Council points to pending cost-dampening reforms rather than any near-term relief, and cantonal premium subsidies offset the burden only for those who qualify. - <a id="st-28d8a985400c" class="st-a"></a>**The Council of States approved the most contested wage-protection clause of the Bilaterals III package with the EU on Wednesday morning 30 September, by 26 votes to 19.** Bilaterals III is the negotiated set of treaties recasting Switzerland's relationship with the EU; the "accompanying measures" that protect Swiss wage levels are its most politically sensitive part. The clause the senators backed strengthens protection against dismissal for elected staff representatives (worker delegates), following the Federal Council's line and against opposition from part of the political right. Pierre-Yves Maillard (Social Democrat, Vaud), who heads the Swiss Trade Union Federation (USS), was its principal defender; supporters argued that dropping it would erode union backing for the whole EU deal. Why it matters: the wage-protection measures are the price of the unions' support for Bilaterals III, so each one the chambers keep intact makes the overall package likelier to survive to a popular vote. [Le Temps, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.letemps.ch/articles/les-elus-du-personnel-mieux-proteges-le-conseil-des-etats-valide-la-14e-mesure-des-bilaterales-iii)
