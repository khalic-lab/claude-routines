- <a id="st-d2f48ff964ad" class="st-a"></a>**Swiss inflation rose to 1.5% in October, the Federal Statistical Office (FSO) said on Tuesday 3 November. It is the highest annual rate since December 2023 and above economists' forecasts of 1.1–1.4%.** The consumer price index rose 0.2% on the month, to 101.7 points (December 2025 = 100). The FSO says the main drivers were heating oil, petrol and diesel, and SRF reports that oil products cost 40% more than a year earlier. Prices for doctors' services, package holidays and hotels fell. On the same day, UBS published its annual wage survey of about 400 Swiss companies. It expects pay to rise by about 1.0% in most sectors in 2027, with retail at 0.8% and watches and jewellery at 0.7%, and puts the real gain after inflation at only 0.2%. Why it matters: energy is pushing up living costs faster than expected, while employers plan only small raises, so household purchasing power will barely grow next year.
