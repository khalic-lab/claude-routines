- <a id="st-a7540d8bddcd" class="st-a"></a>**The federal government confirmed on Thursday 8 October that personal data from Publica, the federal employees' pension fund, leaked after a cyberattack on one of its software suppliers.** The supplier, PK Softech AG, which builds software for pension funds, detected the attack at the end of September and filed a criminal complaint. The Office of the Attorney General has opened an investigation. According to Publica's letter to its members, which SRF has seen, the leaked data may include names, addresses, AHV numbers (the Swiss social-security number), salaries, pension savings and details of spouses or partners. It is not yet clear whether all of the roughly 66,000 active members and 40,000 pensioners are affected. Publica says the pension money itself is safe, and it is warning members to be careful with unusual calls or messages. It is also unclear whether other pension funds that use PK Softech are affected. Why it matters: AHV numbers combined with salary and address data are useful for identity fraud, and the same supplier may serve other Swiss pension funds.
