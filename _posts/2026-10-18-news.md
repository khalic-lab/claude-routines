- <a id="st-46cbaa8e62d8" class="st-a"></a>**Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein signed a free trade agreement with Vietnam in Hanoi on Sunday 18 October, more than a decade after talks began in 2012.** President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin signed for Switzerland as head of the economics department, and Vietnam's Minister of Industry and Trade signed for Vietnam. The agreement removes or cuts customs duties and also covers services, investment, intellectual property, public procurement and sustainable development. Trade between the four EFTA countries and Vietnam was worth €4.8 billion in 2025, with a €2.5 billion surplus in Vietnam's favour (Swiss figures exclude gold). EFTA's main exports to Vietnam are electrical machinery, fish and medicines; its main imports are electrical machinery, shoes and clothing. On Saturday 17 October Parmelin met President Tô Lâm, and both sides said they want to finish talks on a new investment-protection agreement. Why it matters: before it takes effect, the deal needs approval by the Swiss parliament, and it can be challenged by referendum, as happened this week with the EFTA agreement with Malaysia.
