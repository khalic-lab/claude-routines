## 🇨🇭 Switzerland & Vaud

- <a id="st-8900fe8b7b8f" class="st-a"></a>**Former Federal Councillor Christoph Blocher on Friday 27 November called the SVP's official two-man ticket for the 9 December Federal Council election a "Betriebsunfall" (an accident), and hinted that a candidate elected outside it might not be expelled from the party.** Speaking on his YouTube programme "Teleblocher", the 86-year-old said both nominees, Thomas Aeschi and Michael Graber, were "capable and electable", but regretted that neither comes from French-speaking Switzerland and said he would have preferred Bernese cantonal minister Pierre Alain Schnegg alongside Aeschi. SVP statutes bar anyone who accepts a Federal Council seat without the parliamentary group's nomination from party membership. Blocher said Schnegg could still be readmitted by a two-thirds vote of the party executive and the group, but ruled that out for Geneva National Councillor Céline Amaudruz. He did not call for a "wild" election, and warned other parties against one. That rule exists because of Blocher himself: parliament voted him out in 2007 in favour of Eveline Widmer-Schlumpf, who had not been on the SVP ticket. SVP National Councillor Michael Götte defended the group's secret-ballot nomination as "a clean process". SP and FDP members told SRF their positions were unchanged, and the Centre group said it would hear only the two official candidates. ([SRF, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/bundesratswahlen-ausgerechnet-blocher-deutet-hintertuer-fuer-wilde-wahl-an); [Le Temps, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.letemps.ch/suisse/christoph-blocher-desavoue-le-ticket-officiel-udc-pour-le-conseil-federal); [SRF, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/ticket-als-betriebsunfall-so-reagiert-die-politik-auf-blochers-aeusserungen))
  - Why it matters: if parliament keeps to the ticket, French-speaking Switzerland drops to one seat in the seven-member government when Vaud's Guy Parmelin leaves. Blocher's remarks give the parties looking for an alternative an opening twelve days before the vote.

The budget comes ahead of the OSCE ministerial council that Switzerland hosts in Lugano on 3–4 December, where the 57 states will discuss European security and the organisation's ability to act. ([OSCE, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.osce.org/secretariat/670236) [official PR] [new source]; [FDFA via admin.ch, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.admin.ch/fr/newnsb/AdWA1eT6O7Q5) [official PR])
  - Why it matters: OSCE election monitoring and field missions in Ukraine's neighbourhood and the Balkans depend on this money. Holding a consensus budget together is the main concrete test of the Swiss chairmanship before it ends this year.

- <a id="st-561005a669c2" class="st-a"></a>**Geneva will next year start prescribing cocaine, smoked in a supervised inhalation room, to 10 to 20 people severely dependent on crack, Le Temps reported on Friday 27 November.** The pilot runs at CAAP Arve, an addiction unit of Geneva University Hospitals (HUG). ([Le Temps, 27 Nov 2026](https://www.letemps.ch/suisse/geneve/pour-lutter-contre-le-crack-geneve-se-lance-dans-la-prescription-de-cocaine-une-premiere-mondiale)) [single-source]
