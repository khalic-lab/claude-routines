### F1 confirms a 24-race 2027 calendar — with a first-ever Monaco sprint {#st-6acbd46cfe92}
**[Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-reveals-calendar-for-2027-season-with-10-sprint-events.5J8ePLyjRuMyWEIDNUKgY4)** · calendar announcement · Wed 16 September
With no race this week, the headline news came off-track: Formula 1 and the FIA published the 2027 schedule — 24 Grands Prix across 22 countries — and expanded the Sprint format (a short Saturday race that awards points, separate from the Grand Prix) from six weekends to ten. The eye-catcher is Monaco, which will stage a sprint for the first time. That is a provocative choice: the Monte Carlo street circuit is famous for being almost impossible to overtake on, so a race designed to manufacture wheel-to-wheel action is being handed the calendar's least overtaking-friendly venue. *Why it matters:* F1's own figures say Sprint weekends draw 12% more viewers, so the expansion is a commercial bet; whether a Monaco sprint delivers a spectacle or a procession will be the early test of whether ten sprints is one too many.
