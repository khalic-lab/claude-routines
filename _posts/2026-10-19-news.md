- <a id="st-60b35bbd9bbb" class="st-a"></a>**Internal Securitas data show more than 20,000 breaches of Swiss labour law at its central Switzerland branch from 2023 to 2025, affecting more than 900 staff, SRF reported on Monday 19 October.** SRF's investigative and data teams checked records from the security company's Lucerne regional office. One shift can count as several breaches, and Securitas says it cannot match the 20,000 figure. Staff at federal asylum centres were affected too: at Glaubenberg (Obwalden), about 80 employees worked at least 1,800 hours more than allowed. Why it matters: companies bidding for public contracts only have to declare themselves that they follow labour law, and two labour-law experts quoted by SRF say the authorities have to step in.
