### Switzerland's golden Euros: three athletics titles in Birmingham {#st-47507a3e86b1}
**[Joseph roars to gold for Switzerland](https://www.european-athletics.com/news/mens-110m-hurdles-report-joseph-roars-to-gold-for-switzerland)** `[new source]` · European Athletics Championships, Birmingham · 11–16 August · European Athletics
The first European Athletics Championships ever staged in Great Britain produced Switzerland's finest return at the event: three gold medals and seven in total (three gold, three silver, one bronze), sixth on the final medal table. The golds spanned three very different disciplines — Jason Joseph took the men's 110m hurdles on Wednesday 12 August in a season's-best 13.12 seconds; Angelica Moser retained her women's pole vault title on Thursday 13 August with a season's-best 4.80m; and Audrey Werro won the women's 800m on Friday 14 August in a championship-record [1:54.81](https://www.european-athletics.com/news/womens-800m-report-werro-holds-off-hodgkinson-in-a-800m-thriller), holding off Britain's Olympic champion Keely Hodgkinson down the home straight. Simon Ehammer (long jump) and Dominic Lokinyomo Lobalu (10,000m) added silvers.

The wider championship went to the wire: hosts Great Britain, powered by Amy Hunt's unprecedented four golds (100m, 200m, women's 4×100m and the mixed 4×100m relay in a European record 39.97 seconds) and Dina Asher-Smith becoming the most decorated athlete in the competition's 92-year history, were pipped to top of the medal table by Italy — who snatched the meet's final gold in the men's 4×400m relay by 0.03 seconds.
*Why it matters:* Switzerland is not a country that routinely wins European golds in a hurdles, a jump and a two-lap race in the same week. Beating a reigning Olympic 800m champion for one of them — with a championship record, no less — is the standout: it points to genuine depth in Swiss track and field heading into the rest of the outdoor season.
