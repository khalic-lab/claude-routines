- <a id="st-87dbfac527eb" class="st-a"></a>**The mortgage reference rate that governs how much rent Swiss landlords can charge stays at its record low of 1.25%, the Federal Housing Office (BWO) confirmed on Tuesday 1 September.** The benchmark — a nationwide average of interest on outstanding mortgages — last fell in September 2025, from 1.50% to 1.25%, after an earlier cut from 1.75% in March 2025. The tenants' association (MVS) stresses that many renters have still not claimed the reductions they are already owed — a Zürcher Kantonalbank analysis found only about 12% of tenants had requested the second cut by April 2026 — worth on average roughly CHF 65 a month, though tenants must file the request themselves and landlords can offset it against higher costs or inflation. **Why it matters:** for anyone renting in Switzerland this is a live, unclaimed saving, not an automatic one — the rate holding low means the outstanding reduction requests remain valid, but only for tenants who actually lodge them.
