- <a id="st-6127f2f72286" class="st-a"></a>**The COP31 presidency released a new draft final decision in Antalya early on Sunday 22 November that still names no exit from coal, oil or gas, and the EU and France rejected it as the summit ran more than 40 hours past its scheduled Friday close.** The roughly ten-page text, prepared by the Turkish and Australian co-presidencies, refers only in passing to the 2023 Dubai decision (COP28) to "transition away" from fossil fuels. It acknowledges for the first time that warming will overshoot the Paris Agreement's 1.5°C goal, aiming to limit how far and for how long. It also calls for faster use of carbon capture, utilisation and storage (CCUS, technology that traps CO₂ from smokestacks, promoted by oil and gas companies) and a Turkish goal of raising electricity's share of global final energy use to 35% by 2035. French minister Monique Barbut called it "unacceptable" and said it "takes us backwards". Ireland's Darragh O'Brien, speaking for the EU presidency, said the chances of a deal were falling "by the hour", and an EU official told AFP there was a "very real possibility" of no agreement. COP president Murat Kurum said "the ball is now in the countries' court". Observers at the closed talks said Saudi Arabia and other oil producers pushed to keep fossil fuels out. The plenary to adopt the text was postponed repeatedly on Sunday morning, and many negotiators have already left, which raises the risk that too few are present to adopt it.
