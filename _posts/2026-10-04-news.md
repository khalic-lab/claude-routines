After the city appealed to the Confederation in 2024 over planning and legal uncertainty, the Federal Office of Culture convened the parties and brokered a solution: ISOS will in future be applied directly only where a project genuinely affects the character of a townscape. **Why it matters:** housing shortage and construction costs are among the sharpest everyday pressures in Swiss cities, and narrowing a federal conservation rule that had frozen thousands of flats is a concrete lever on supply — though it trades some heritage protection for building capacity.
