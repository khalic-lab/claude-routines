- <a id="st-1f143744fa62" class="st-a"></a>**The Swiss Federal Audit Office (CDF/EFK) found on Monday 12 October that half of all pesticide approval applications filed between 2018 and 2025 are still pending.** Approval is the federal clearance a plant-protection product needs before it can be sold. Le Temps puts the figure at 557 of 1,117 applications, and 81% of those filed in 2024. Since 2018 the Federal Food Safety and Veterinary Office (FSVO/OSAV) has closed fewer files each year than it received, and emergency authorisations have filled the gap: 34 in 2024, against 18 ordinary approvals. The auditors blame a process split across five federal offices in three departments, no binding deadlines, digitisation that is still ineffective, and fees that cover only 15% of costs. The three responsible federal councillors have asked for an action plan to clear the backlog. Why it matters: emergency authorisations, granted without the full ordinary review, have become the main way pesticides reach Swiss fields. The auditors want binding deadlines and higher fees so the ordinary review is used again.
