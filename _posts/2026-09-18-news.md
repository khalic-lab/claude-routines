- <a id="st-4aeeca4c21d3" class="st-a"></a>**The Federal Administrative Court issued its first-ever ruling on health insurers' new right to challenge cantonal hospital planning on Friday 18 September, rejecting a complaint by the insurers' association Prio.Swiss against the canton of Schwyz.** Since 2024 insurer associations have held a Verbandsbeschwerderecht (a collective right of appeal) meant to let them police cantonal hospital lists and curb over-provision and costs; Prio.Swiss used it to argue Schwyz should coordinate its three hospitals' offerings better across cantonal borders. The court declared the appeal inadmissible without ruling on the substance, holding that a whole hospital list cannot be challenged as such — only individual service mandates (the specific treatments a hospital is licensed to provide).
