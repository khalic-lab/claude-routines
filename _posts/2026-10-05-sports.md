**[F1 Bahrain GP in Malaysia: Verstappen wins chaotic race from Antonelli as Russell retires (Autosport)](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-bahrain-gp-in-malaysia-report/10861849/)** · Formula 1 "Bahrain Grand Prix", Sepang, Malaysia · Sun 2026-10-04
Max Verstappen (Red Bull) took his first victory of 2026, ahead of championship leader Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) and Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari, his first podium since Silverstone). The more important result is for the title: Antonelli's team-mate and closest title rival George Russell, who had started seventh and run second, retired with a power-unit failure while on course for a podium, so Antonelli's lead over him grew to 84 points (320 to 236, from the cross-checked standings) with seven races to go — a win is worth 25. *Why it matters:* Reliability, not speed, is now the main threat to Antonelli's lead, and the "unacceptable" electronics glitch (drivers' word, not the FIA's) is a worry the sport must answer before the next wet race.

### Elfyn Evans is world rally champion — by one corner {#wrc-evans}
**[WRC Sardinia: Evans claims historic title after Solberg crashes in final-stage showdown (Autosport)](https://www.autosport.com/wrc/news/wrc-sardinia-elfyn-evans-claims-historic-title-after-oliver-solberg-crashes-in-final-stage-showdown/10861985/)** · World Rally Championship final round, Sardinia · Sun 2026-10-04
Toyota's Elfyn Evans became Britain's first world rally champion since Richard Burns in 2001 after fellow Toyota driver Oliver Solberg ran off the road two corners from the end of the closing Power Stage (a short televised stage that carries bonus points). The two were level on points going into it, with Solberg set to win on countback (the tie-break by wins) if they finished level, so Evans needed to beat him on the stage. Evans, who had punctured and finished the rally only fifth, said he was "quite sure it was over". Solberg still won the rally itself — his third win of the year — by 57.6 seconds, but lost the title.
*Why it matters:* A championship settled by a single mistake in the very last minutes of the season is the clearest reminder that rallying's points are about the whole weekend, not the event win.
